Landowners

Residential land in Utah.

ZAKO is interested in learning about residential land that may fit its own investment and acquisition strategy. If you own land in Utah County or along the Wasatch Front, we would be glad to hear from you.

Tell us about your land

What to expect

Careful, unhurried and clear.

  1. Share the basics

    Email us the address or parcel number and a little about your plans. There is no cost.

  2. We study the property

    We review public information such as zoning, access, utilities and terrain.

  3. We follow up

    If the property may fit our strategy, we will tell you and ask whether you would like to talk further. If not, we will say so.

Your land

Start with the basics.

  • Contacting us creates no obligation to sell.
  • You are welcome to involve your own agent, attorney or advisor at any point.
  • We do not share your property information without your permission.

ZAKO is not a real estate brokerage. We do not represent landowners, list or market property, or find buyers for others. Any transaction would depend on due diligence, legal review and a separate written agreement signed by both parties.
