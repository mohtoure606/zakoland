How it works

Simple, unhurried and clear.

You do not need to know anything about development to start. Tell us a little about your property and we will take it from there.

  1. 01

    Start a conversation

    Share the address or parcel number and a little about your plans. There is no cost and no obligation.

  2. 02

    We take a careful look

    We review public information about the property and what builders in the area are looking for.

  3. 03

    We talk about options

    If there may be a fit, we explain what we see and what a next step could look like. If not, we tell you.

Good to know

  • We do not represent you

    ZAKO is not a real estate agent, and we do not list or market your property.

  • Bring your own advisors

    You are welcome to involve your own agent, attorney or advisor at any point.

  • Your information stays private

    We share property details with others only with your permission.

  • Nothing is final until it is in writing

    A conversation creates no obligation. Any transaction would need a written agreement you have reviewed and signed.
