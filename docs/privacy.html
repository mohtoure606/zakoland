Privacy Policy

Draft for attorney review. This policy is not in effect until it is finalized.

This policy explains what ZAKO Land Partners ("ZAKO", "we") collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • What you send through our forms: name, company, email, phone, property details and messages.
  • Basic technical data our hosting provider records, such as IP address and pages requested.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and evaluate the property or criteria you shared.
  • To contact you about land opportunities related to your inquiry.
  • To keep business records and run the website securely.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that run this site and its forms (website hosting through GitHub Pages, and Formspree, which delivers form submissions to our email), with your permission, or when the law requires. We share property details with builders only with the owner's permission.

Email

Every marketing email we send will include a way to opt out, and we will honor your request promptly.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, contact team@zakoland.com.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Children

This site is for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Effective date: set when finalized.
