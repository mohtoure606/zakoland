About ZAKO

A Utah land company with an engineer's eye.

ZAKO identifies residential land opportunities and connects them with the builders shaping Utah's communities. We do the research first, so the right opportunities reach the right people.

Founder

Why I started ZAKO

I am originally from Mali, and Utah has become home. It is where I studied civil engineering.

Before entering engineering, I worked on survey crews across Utah, gaining firsthand experience with property boundaries, land measurement and the early stages of residential development. Today, my work in civil engineering includes water and irrigation projects.

These experiences shaped how I understand land. Its potential goes beyond acreage or location. Access, topography, drainage, water, utilities and surrounding development all play an important role in what a property may become.

I founded ZAKO to bring that perspective to residential land investment and acquisition. Our focus is to identify promising land, understand the factors that shape its potential, and connect it with the builders and developers who may be the right fit.

We believe good land decisions begin with careful research, clear communication and strong professional relationships.

Mohamed ToureFounder, ZAKO Land Partners

What we believe

Principles we work by

  • Demand comes first

    We learn what builders need before we look for land.

  • Research before promises

    We separate what we know from what still needs checking, and we say so.

  • The right expertise

    Specialized questions go to qualified, independent professionals.

  • Utah first

    We are starting where we know the ground, and growing with the people we work with.

How we work

Five steps. No shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Understand the opportunity

    We start with the property, its location and ownership, and the goals of the people involved.

  2. 02

    Research the land

    We review available information on access, zoning, topography, utilities, water and surrounding development.

  3. 03

    Bring the right expertise

    When a question needs a specialist, qualified independent professionals can help with surveying, engineering, land use, title and legal matters.

  4. 04

    Connect the opportunity

    We look for builders and developers whose needs may align with the property.

  5. 05

    Move forward carefully

    When there is mutual interest, the parties evaluate the opportunity and decide next steps with appropriate professional and transaction support.
