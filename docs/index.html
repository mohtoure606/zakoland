How we work

Five steps. No shortcuts.

A clear sequence from first look to the right connection, with the right people involved at each step.

  1. 01

    Understand the opportunity

    We start with the property, its location and ownership, and the goals of the people involved.

  2. 02

    Research the land

    We review available information on access, zoning, topography, utilities, water and surrounding development.

  3. 03

    Bring the right expertise

    When a question needs a specialist, qualified independent professionals can help with surveying, engineering, land use, title and legal matters.

  4. 04

    Connect the opportunity

    We look for builders and developers whose needs may align with the property.

  5. 05

    Move forward carefully

    When there is mutual interest, the parties evaluate the opportunity and decide next steps with appropriate professional and transaction support.

Our network

A strong network. A thoughtful process.

Residential land opportunities often need more than one perspective. We are building relationships with experienced real estate, surveying, engineering, land use, title, legal and financing professionals, so the right expertise can join the process when it is needed.

  • Builders and developers

    Researched land opportunities and organized property information to help you evaluate a potential acquisition.

  • Landowners

    A professional, no-pressure starting point for a conversation about your land.

  • Real estate professionals

    A working relationship with a team that does its homework, built around appropriate, properly structured transactions.

  • Engineers and surveyors

    Specialized expertise matters. Qualified independent firms can be engaged when a site needs it.

  • Title, legal, planning and financing

    Organized opportunities where the right expertise can help a transaction come together properly.

ZAKO does not provide real estate brokerage, engineering, surveying, legal, title or financing services. Those services are provided by independent, qualified professionals when they are separately engaged.
