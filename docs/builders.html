Building relationships with Utah’s homebuilders.

ZAKO is a residential land investment company. As we build our acquisition strategy, we want to understand how Utah homebuilders and developers think about land: where they build, what they need and what makes a site work.

Why it matters to us

Land decisions start with demand.

  1. Understand the market

    Knowing what homebuilders look for helps us judge which land is worth our time and capital.

  2. Research with discipline

    We evaluate zoning, access, utilities, terrain and surrounding growth before pursuing any property.

  3. Build for the long term

    We value relationships that grow over years, not single transactions.
